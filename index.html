How it works for local businesses

🔨
Shops bid from a penny
Chamber members get an email invite to the weekly token auction. Bidding starts at $0.01 and runs to a fixed date.
📍
Winners hit the board
The winning business gets a token on the board for the season. Premium corners auction separately.
📣
Players tap the ads
Land on a token and the business's interactive ad pops up: offers, hours, tap-to-call. Extra ad upgrades sold in-game.
